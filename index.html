About

Who I Am

I'm a Senior Mechatronics Engineering student at the Lebanese American University, driven by a deep curiosity about how intelligent machines interact with the physical world. My focus spans control systems, robotics, and embedded systems, where mathematics meets hardware.

My current research, an international collaboration between LAU and UMLP (France), explores programmable matter, designing modular robots that can reconfigure themselves to perform different tasks. I'm specifically developing a novel actuation system for the Datom MRR platform.

Outside the lab, I've invested 300+ hours tutoring engineering and mathematics, served as Vice President of the Student Council, and competed in national robotics competitions. I believe the best engineers can teach, lead, and build.
